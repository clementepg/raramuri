## Water recording

Jesusita Loya Guerra; Gabriela Caballero (2022). *Audio files accompanying A grammar of Choguita Rarámuri.* Source file `co1234[06_044-06_053].wav`, linked from p. 202, example (2e). Distributed here as `water-choguita.wav`, without alteration.

- Dataset: https://zenodo.org/records/7268366
- Original file: https://zenodo.org/api/records/7268366/files/co1234%5B06_044-06_053%5D.wav/content

No greeting or ball recording is supplied. No synthetic Rarámuri speech is used. The water illustration depicts a vessel; the Rarámuri word is the word for water, not a translation of cup.
