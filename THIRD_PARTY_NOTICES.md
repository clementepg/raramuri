Written companion forms were checked against Cambridge Dictionary and MDBG/CC-CEDICT; links and context notes appear per word in bridge-data.js. Elote is a fresh maize cob; Corn/maïs/mais/Mais/玉米 are broader names for maize shown as a fresh cob. Mandarin offer responses use 要 (want) and 不要 (do not want), rather than implying a universal one-word yes/no.

The 50-concept family curriculum is an editorial starting library, not a diagnostic test, deadline or universal required word list. Developmental sources are linked in the family guide: ASHA 13–18 and 19–24 month communication guidance, NIDCD speech/language development, and AAP/ASHA multilingual-family guidance. It prioritizes caregiver interaction with real objects and accepts gestures, sounds and word attempts.

Ten new illustrations are fictional and AI-generated with the built-in image generation tool. WebP display derivatives are optimized for delivery; original PNGs remain locally under output/imagegen/first-words. Prompt set and asset names: IMAGE_PROMPTS.md.

Device speech, when available, is synthesized by the browser/device and is labelled separately from speaker recordings. It is offered only for Spanish, English, French, Italian, German and Mandarin, never Rarámuri. No recorded-speaker licence or human attribution is claimed for synthesized speech. Device voices may need a network connection and are not part of the offline recording pack.

Family recordings are personal, adult-recorded material stored in the local browser. They are not shipped with this repository or uploaded by this app. Family entries are labelled separately and do not acquire the published sources’ verification or licences. A family controls its own backup files.

The additional 41 illustrations were generated in separate built-in image generation requests. Full prompts and project asset names are in IMAGE_PROMPTS.md. PNG originals are retained locally in output/imagegen/everyday-v7; delivery WebP derivatives are 768px quality90. Three older large PNGs also have smaller WebP delivery derivatives; their originals remain intact.

The parent explanation is original project wording for family discussion, not a Rarámuri community endorsement. It draws on [UNESCO’s multilingual education position](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/education-multilingual-world-unesco-education-position-paper), [UNESCO’s community-led knowledge transmission guidance](https://www.unesco.org/en/links/transmission) and [INALI’s Ralámuli language profile](https://www.inali.gob.mx/detalle/lengua-ralamuli-tarahumara). It makes no IQ, employment, diagnostic or guaranteed educational-outcome claim.
