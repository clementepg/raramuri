## Water recording

Jesusita Loya Guerra; Gabriela Caballero (2022). *Audio files accompanying A grammar of Choguita Rarámuri.* Source file `co1234[06_044-06_053].wav`, linked from p. 202, example (2e). Distributed here as `water-choguita.wav`, without alteration.

- Dataset: https://zenodo.org/records/7268366
- Original file: https://zenodo.org/api/records/7268366/files/co1234%5B06_044-06_053%5D.wav/content
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

No Rarámuri greeting or ball recording is supplied. No synthetic Rarámuri speech is used. The water illustration depicts a vessel; the Rarámuri word is the word for water, not a translation of cup.

## Illustrations

The three fictional family illustrations were generated with the built-in image generator for this project. They are design concepts, not documentary images of named people or an assertion of approval by a community.
